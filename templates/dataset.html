MedicineNet condition index

MedicineNet is copyright WebMD and its robots.txt disallows the AI crawlers, so only the public condition index is stored — the name, slug and link. No article text or image is copied in. Follow a link to read the article at the source.

DeepLesion CT annotations

Radiology ground truth from the NIH Clinical Center: one row per annotated lesion, with its bounding box, RECIST size, coarse lesion type and patient demographics. This is imaging research data, not symptom text, so it is kept in its own sheet and never mixed into the symptom classifier's training. The readme states no redistribution licence; the ~238 GB image archive is not downloaded.
