How it works

  1. Describe Type your symptoms in any of 22 Indian languages, or upload a photo. Your language is detected automatically.
  2. Understand Likely conditions are ranked with a confidence score, and severity is graded from self-care to emergency.
  3. Act safely Medicine suggestions are filtered against your allergies, conditions and anything you already take, then laid out on a timed schedule.
  4. Plan the day A daily routine is built around your medicine times, hydration target and sleep need.
